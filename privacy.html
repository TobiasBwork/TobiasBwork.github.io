Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Jarvis is a private personal assistant used by one person, its owner. This policy explains what it does with Google account data.

What Jarvis accesses

  • Google Calendar (read and write): to show the owner's schedule, find free time, and add, move or remove events the owner asks for.
  • Gmail (read-only): to search, read and summarise the owner's emails. Jarvis cannot send, delete or change emails.

How the data is used

  • Data is used only to provide the assistant to its owner.
  • To understand requests and write replies, relevant calendar and email content is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for processing.
  • Jarvis stores its chat history, to-do list and notes about the owner's preferences on the owner's own server. Google login tokens are stored there too.
  • Data is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than the assistant itself.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke Jarvis's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete all stored data by deleting it from their server.

Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on Jarvis's Google sign-in screen.
